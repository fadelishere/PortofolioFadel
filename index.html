About Me

I am Fadel Naya Raflinanto — an Informatics Engineering student at Dian Nuswantoro University with a profound interest in graphic design and web design. I focus on creating attractive, modern, and user-friendly visual interfaces, both for websites and other digital media. I am accustomed to combining design aesthetics with user experience (UI/UX) to create works that are not only beautiful but also highly functional.

Furthermore, I actively develop my skills in using design tools and keep up with the latest design trends to produce relevant and innovative outcomes. With a strong enthusiasm for learning, I continuously seek opportunities to grow, explore my creativity, and make a positive impact in the digital design realm.

Work with heart, stay grateful, and keep smiling.
